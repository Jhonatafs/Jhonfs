## Challenges

A **challenge** is a set of tasks created to offer the group a personal-development practice. It may come from a shared goal or from an experience that a member considers useful for anyone who wants to grow in discipline, study, work, health, self-knowledge, or life direction. Creating a challenge means sharing a concrete exercise with the guild, so others can practice it, test it, and progress through it.

### Active Challenges

* **Imago Vitae Expletae** [`@JhonFs`](https://habitica.com/profile/9cfd1c50-fa73-4b9c-b226-af8cb9c7696e)

  Goal: help members reflect on their goals, life aims, and personal direction.

### Rules for Creating Challenges

* The challenge must have a clear goal.
* The challenge must be connected to the guild’s focus: study, discipline, work, health, organization, projects, or personal development.
* Avoid impossible, humiliating, dangerous, or purposeless challenges.
* Do not create challenges based on sharing personal data, passwords, documents, money, or sensitive information.
* Do not create challenges that encourage excess, extreme deprivation, physical punishment, conflict, or disrespect.
* Every challenge should have simple, verifiable, and realistic tasks.
* The creator should follow the challenge and answer questions when possible.
* Abandoned, confusing, or off-purpose challenges may be removed.
